Wordbound

Wordbound is a simple word-guessing game. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, and does not require an internet connection. It saves your game statistics (wins, losses, streaks, daily puzzle progress) locally on your device using standard browser storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer or any third party. The app uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

These apps do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
