--- title: Privacy Policy --- # Privacy Policy Last updated: October 2026 This privacy policy applies to all Android apps published by Aloxen (developer account: Aloxen). Each app is covered individually below. --- ## Wordbound Wordbound is a word-guessing game. The game itself runs on your device and makes no network requests of its own. The developer does not operate any server for Wordbound and does not receive, collect, or have access to your personal data. ### Information stored on your device Wordbound saves the following on your device only, using standard browser storage inside the app: your game progress and statistics (wins, losses, streaks, daily puzzle progress, coins, unlocked appearances, and achievements), your settings, and your profile (a display name and an avatar or a photo you choose). A photo you choose is resized and kept only on your device. None of this leaves your device through the app. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. ### Advertising (Google AdMob) Wordbound shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner ad, and optional rewarded ads that you can choose to watch to get hints, coins, or appearance unlocks. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interaction and diagnostic data. This information is collected and processed by Google, not by the developer. - How Google uses information from apps that use its services: - Google Privacy Policy: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (usually under Settings, then Privacy or Google, then Ads; the exact path varies by device). ### Consent Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), Wordbound shows Google's consent form before requesting ads. You can change your choice at any time using the privacy options button in the app's settings when it is shown. If you do not consent, ads may be limited or not shown, and the game continues to work normally. ### Google Play Games (optional) You may connect Wordbound to Google Play Games Services from the You page. If you do, Google shares your Play Games player ID and display name with the app, and Wordbound saves your achievements to your Google account through Google Play Games Services. Connecting is optional; if you do not connect, nothing is sent. Google handles this data under its own privacy policy and Play Games Services terms, and you can manage it in the Google Play Games app. ### Internet access and permissions The app requests internet and network-state access only so that ads and Google Play Games can work. The game can be played offline. The app does not ask for your contacts, location, microphone, or camera. Choosing a profile photo uses Android's file picker, so the app only receives the single photo you select. ### What we do not do We do not sell your personal data, and we do not run our own analytics or tracking tools. Because the developer does not hold your data, requests to access or delete data held by Google (for advertising or Google Play Games) should be made to Google through its privacy tools. --- ## Children's Privacy Wordbound is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. ## Changes to This Policy Updates will be posted at this same URL. ## Contact For questions about this policy, contact: kingspeedx11@gmail.com
